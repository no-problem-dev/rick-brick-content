#### Life Sciences & Drug Discovery AI
A research team at Cincinnati Children's Hospital has developed a technology to generate functional human gut organoids, including native nerve cells, using a proprietary "Confined culture system." This research was published in *Nature Biomedical Engineering*. While traditional culture methods required complex additional manipulations to functionally mature tissues, the new technology uses a 3D-printed scaffold that allows the organoids to grow twice as fast to a size suitable for transplantation. This is expected to improve the accuracy of drug toxicity testing and lead to future applications in compensatory medical treatment for organ function.

#### Energy Engineering & Climate Science
A new study published in *Science Advances* reveals that due to anthropogenic climate change, nighttime cooling is no longer as reliably the case as it once was. Over the past 50 years, the amount of time weather conditions have been conducive to wildfires has increased by about 36% across much of the continents. In some parts of the Western United States, in particular, the duration of active nighttime fire behavior has significantly lengthened, diminishing the traditional advantage of a "nighttime break" for firefighting efforts. This trend necessitates a fundamental review of fire management strategies and indicates that climate change is directly impacting not just temperature rise but also regional operational capabilities.
